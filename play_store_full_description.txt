VocaGuard protects you and your loved ones from phone scams by analyzing your calls in real time and warning you the moment it detects a scam — before any money or personal details are handed over. It is built especially for seniors and the family members who help protect them.

HOW IT WORKS
When a call arrives, VocaGuard screens the conversation and checks it against known scam patterns — fake bank and government calls, tech-support fraud, prize and lottery scams, investment and cryptocurrency scams, and more. If it detects a scam, it shows a clear on-screen warning during the call so you can hang up in time. Every call is saved to your history so you can review it later.

FAMILY GUARD MODE
Worried about an elderly parent? Turn on Family Guard. When a scam is detected on their phone, VocaGuard automatically sends an SMS alert to a family caregiver you choose — so help is never far away.

KEY FEATURES
- Real-time scam detection during live calls
- Clear on-screen scam warnings
- Caregiver SMS alerts (Family Guard mode)
- Call history with transcripts
- Contact-aware: skips screening for people you already know
- In-call keypad for navigating phone menus

WHY VOCAGUARD NEEDS EACH PERMISSION
- Microphone and foreground service: to analyze call audio live during a call and keep call monitoring active while you are on the phone. A persistent notification is shown whenever monitoring is running.
- Phone and call permissions (phone state, answer calls, call phone): to detect, screen, and handle incoming calls — the core purpose of the app.
- SMS: only in Family Guard mode, to send a scam-alert text to the caregiver you designate when a scam is detected on the user's phone.
- Contacts: to skip scam screening for numbers already saved in your contacts.

VocaGuard runs quietly in the background and never records or shares your calls for any purpose other than detecting scams to protect you.
