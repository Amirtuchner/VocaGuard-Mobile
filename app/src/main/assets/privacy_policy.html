VocaGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-24

1. What VocaGuard Does

VocaGuard is a scam-call detection app. It screens incoming calls and, with your permission, transcribes audio during active calls to detect scam patterns.

2. Data Collected

  • Phone numbers — checked against local and community databases.
  • Call audio — transcribed locally on-device using Android's SpeechRecognizer API. Audio is never sent off-device by VocaGuard.
  • Call transcripts — stored locally in an encrypted Room database. The 50 most recent transcripts are retained; older ones are automatically deleted.
  • Settings — sensitivity level, locale preference, and whitelist are stored in SharedPreferences on your device.

3. Data Shared With Third Parties

  • NumVerify API (optional) — if you provide an API key, phone numbers are sent to numverify.com for reputation lookup over HTTPS. You control whether this key is set.
  • Community blocklist (optional) — if you tap "Sync Now", your device downloads a JSON file from the configured URL. No data is uploaded.

VocaGuard does not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash reporters.

4. Permissions Used

  • READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_CALL_LOG, ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS — required to identify the caller before the call is answered.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — required for in-call speech recognition. Audio processing happens entirely on-device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — required to show scam alert notifications.
  • ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE — detects when a call window is active; reads no content from other apps.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (optional) — shows an on-screen warning overlay during a detected scam call. Requires manual grant in Settings.
  • INTERNET — used only for the optional NumVerify lookup and community blocklist download.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored exclusively on your device. You can delete individual transcripts from the History tab at any time. Uninstalling VocaGuard removes all stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

VocaGuard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be reflected in the app's Settings screen with a revised "last updated" date above.

8. Contact

For questions, open an issue at the VocaGuard project repository.
