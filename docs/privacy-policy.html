VocaGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

VocaGuard ("we", "our", or "the app") is a real-time scam call and message detection service operated by Amir Tuchner, Lev Ha'ir 2, Modi'in, Israel, who is the data controller responsible for your personal data. This policy explains what data we process, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and your rights. It covers both VocaGuard users and people who call a VocaGuard user (see Section 9).

1. How VocaGuard Works

When you enable call forwarding, your mobile carrier forwards incoming calls to a VocaGuard phone number provided by our telephony carrier (DIDWW). The call then reaches the VocaGuard server, hosted by Hetzner in Germany. The server alerts your phone, and when you answer in the app the call is connected to you. While the call is in progress the server converts the caller's speech to text and checks it for scam patterns. If a scam is detected, an alert is sent to your phone in real time.

No call audio is stored. Audio is converted to text in memory and discarded immediately. The text is not stored on our server either: it is sent to your phone (see Section 2) and then discarded.

Call monitoring notice: The calling party hears a short recorded notice before the call connects: "This call may be recorded and monitored for fraud protection. By staying on the line, you consent to this." The notice plays when the VocaGuard number your calls forward to is outside Israel (for example, our US number) and whenever the caller has a US or Canadian (+1) number. Calls between Israeli numbers do not play it, because Israel requires only one party's consent. If your own number is outside Israel, you also hear the notice when you answer. By enabling call forwarding to VocaGuard, you accept sole responsibility for compliance with any applicable call recording or monitoring laws in your jurisdiction.

2. Data We Process

On our server

  • Your account — your phone number, a push-notification (FCM) token for your device, a private call extension and its password, the VocaGuard forwarding number assigned to you, the date you first registered (used for your free trial), and technical status flags (for example, whether the app appears to have been uninstalled, and whether your mobile carrier passes on the information needed to route forwarded calls to you).
  • Call audio — processed in memory during the call only, never stored.
  • Call transcripts — created in memory during the call, delivered to your device through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and not stored on the server.
  • Server logs — for operation, security and troubleshooting, the server logs call metadata: the caller's phone number, your number, call times, the detected language, the length of the transcript, and, for calls flagged as scams, the scam type, score and the scam keywords that matched. Logs do not contain what was said. They are deleted automatically after at most 30 days.
  • Scam reports — if you report a number as a scam, we store that number, the scam type and your phone number (so that only you can withdraw your report). Reported numbers and their scam type, without your number, are shared with all VocaGuard users as a community blocklist.
  • Family Guard alerts — when a scam is detected and Family Guard is on, your family contact's phone number and the alert text are sent to our server and passed to Twilio to deliver the SMS or alert call. They are not stored on our server beyond the server logs described above.

On your device only

  • Call history and transcripts — the caller's number, call times and the transcript of each analysed call. The 50 most recent transcripts are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Message scanning — if enabled, the text of incoming WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram and SMS notifications from senders who are not in your contacts is checked on your device. Message text is never sent to our server or stored.
  • Contacts — used on your device to skip known contacts and show caller names. Your contact list is never sent to our server.
  • Settings and Family Guard contacts — stored on your device.

3. Third Parties

  • DIDWW — our telephony carrier. Provides the phone numbers that receive your forwarded calls and carries the call audio to our server.
  • Hetzner — hosts our server in Germany.
  • Google Firebase — Cloud Messaging delivers alerts, call notifications and transcripts to your device; Crashlytics receives crash reports (device model, Android version and technical error details, without call audio, transcripts or phone numbers).
  • Twilio — sends Family Guard SMS messages and alert calls. When you register, we also send your phone number to Twilio's lookup service once to identify your mobile carrier, so calls can be routed to you correctly.
  • Tenjin — measures which advertising campaigns led to app installs, and records app events such as starting a trial or subscribing. Tenjin receives your device's advertising ID and basic device information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Google Play — handles subscriptions and payments. We do not receive your payment details.
  • NumVerify (optional) — only if you enter your own API key, caller numbers are sent to numverify.com for a reputation lookup.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles. These providers act on our behalf and may only use the data to provide their service to us.

4. Permissions Used

  • READ_PHONE_STATE, ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS, CALL_PHONE — detect call state, handle calls, dial the carrier codes that turn call forwarding on and off, and let you call back from your history.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — carries your voice on calls you answer through VocaGuard.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — show incoming-call screens and scam alerts.
  • NOTIFICATION LISTENER (optional) — scan incoming message notifications for scams on your device, as described in Section 2.
  • READ_CONTACTS (optional) — skip known contacts and show caller names.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (optional) — show a warning on top of other apps when a scam is detected.
  • INTERNET, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, foreground services — connect to the VocaGuard server and keep protection running.
  • Advertising ID — used only by Tenjin for install attribution (see Section 3).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Call audio and transcripts — not stored on our server.
  • Server logs — deleted automatically after at most 30 days.
  • Your account data — kept while you use VocaGuard. To delete it, use Delete all my data in the app's Settings, or email support@vocaguard.com. We delete it within 7 days.
  • Scam reports — kept while the number remains on the community blocklist. Deleting your account also deletes your phone number from your reports.
  • On-device data — you can delete individual history records in the app; uninstalling VocaGuard removes all data stored on your device.

6. Data Security

Communication between the app and our server (registration, alerts, settings) uses HTTPS. Calls are encrypted from the point they leave the public phone network: our telephony carrier sends them to our server over TLS with encrypted audio (SRTP), and current versions of the app connect to our server over TLS with encrypted audio as well. The part of a call that travels over the public phone network, before it reaches our carrier, is a regular phone call and is not encrypted; and because our server must analyse the audio, calls are not end-to-end encrypted. Our server is protected by a firewall, key-only administrator access and automatic blocking of repeated failed login attempts.

7. Legal Bases (EU / UK GDPR)

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Providing the service to you (contract) — your account data, call routing, alerts, transcripts and Family Guard alerts you set up.
  • Legitimate interests — analysing calls made to VocaGuard users in order to protect them from fraud; keeping server logs for security and troubleshooting; crash reporting; measuring the effectiveness of our advertising. We keep this processing to what is necessary: no audio or transcript is stored, logs contain no call content and are deleted within 30 days.
  • Legal obligations — where we must keep or disclose data by law.

8. International Transfers

Our server is in Germany (EU). Some providers listed in Section 3 (for example Google, Twilio and Tenjin) may process data in the United States or other countries. Where data leaves the EU, we rely on the provider's safeguards approved under GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. Israel is recognised by the European Commission as providing adequate protection.

9. If You Called a VocaGuard User

If you called someone who uses VocaGuard, your call was forwarded to our server by their choice. Your speech was converted to text in memory to check it for scam patterns; the audio was not stored, and the text was delivered only to the VocaGuard user's phone. Our server logs keep your phone number and the call time for up to 30 days. You can ask us about this processing, ask us to delete your number from our logs, or object to it, by emailing support@vocaguard.com with your phone number.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR, the Israeli Privacy Protection Law or California privacy law), you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy of it. To use these rights, email support@vocaguard.com. We reply within 30 days and will not treat you differently for using them. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children's Privacy

VocaGuard is available to users of all ages. Users under 13 should use the app only with parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes other than providing the service.
